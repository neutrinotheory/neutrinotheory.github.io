# PhD/Postdoc

Positions may become available subject to funding; interested candidates are encouraged to contact us. 

Applications to fellowships from the [Research Foundation – Flanders (FWO)](https://www.fwo.be/en/)—including PhD, junior postdoctoral, and senior postdoctoral—as well as other European funding programs are welcome. The group will provide support throughout the application process.

# Visitors/Seminars
Researchers interested in visiting our group or presenting their work in a seminar are encouraged to get in touch. We are open to hosting research stays and invited talks.
